How Spielfeld works

What you see

For the next Bundesliga Spieltag we predict a best XI for each club from that club’s previous match: who started, who was on the bench, the result, and the next opponent. Open a match for the pitch board.

How much data?

The training table is {{ payload.train_rows or "—" }} player rows from {{ payload.train_matches or "—" }} team-matches. One row is one player who started the previous game or sat on that bench. The label is whether they started the next game. Files: data/xi_matches.csv (one row per team-match) and data/xi_players.csv (the TabPFN table).

Model in use: {{ payload.model or "—" }}. Predictions generated: {{ payload.generated_at or "—" }}.

OpenLigaDB supplies fixtures and results. Transfermarkt supplies the starting XI and the bench after each game. Ratings, market values, and tracking data are not in the table.

After a matchday finishes, the refresh job appends those games and refits TabPFN 3.5 through the Prior Labs API. A run with no new games does not refit.

What is TabPFN?

TabPFN 3.5 is a tabular foundation model. The app calls the Prior Labs API (TabPFN-3.5 via tabpfn-client) and fits one classifier: the chance a player starts, given the last XI.

The best XI is the most likely goalkeeper plus the ten most likely outfield players from last game’s starters and substitutes. Players outside that squad are not candidates.

What is ShapIQ?

ShapIQ (built on the shapiq library) explains a tip in units of contribution: which features pushed the probability up or down for the tipped result.

On the ShapIQ tab you get:

  • Match drivers — team-level factors (ELO gap, squad value, form, …)
  • Players who start — how XI quality is shared across names
  • Line vs line — attack vs defence, midfield battle, star edge, etc.
  • Spieltag what-if — nudge the home XI quality edge across all fixtures

Player / interaction layers are derived from the current XIs and the tip’s ShapIQ budget. They are transparent what-if attributions, not a second full retrain per click.

Lineups & Best XI

The pitch shows the predicted XI. Shirt strength on the board is that player’s predicted start probability. The lab can still swap in someone from the previous bench.

Limits (be honest)

  • Tips are probabilities, not certainties — football is noisy.
  • Training uses historical results + engineered features; injuries, tactics, and referees are only partly captured.
  • Squad-estimate XIs can miss who actually starts on Saturday.
  • Lab swaps use a local sensitivity / ShapIQ approx — not a full model retrain on every click.
