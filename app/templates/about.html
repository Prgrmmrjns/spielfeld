What is TabPFN?

TabPFN is a “tabular prior-data fitted network”: a model family built for spreadsheet-style data (rows of matches, columns of features). Think of it as a strong modern classifier for tables — here, home / draw / away.

We engineer features such as ELO-style ratings, recent form, goals, rest days, head-to-head, squad value, and player XI lines / matchups. TabPFN learns the mapping from those features to home / draw / away probabilities — including the player XI line and matchup features.

What is ShapIQ?

ShapIQ (built on the shapiq library) explains a tip in units of contribution: which features pushed the probability up or down for the tipped result.

On the ShapIQ tab you get:

  • Match drivers — team-level factors (ELO gap, squad value, form, …)
  • Players who start — how XI quality is shared across names
  • Line vs line — attack vs defence, midfield battle, star edge, etc.
  • Spieltag what-if — nudge the home XI quality edge across all fixtures

Player / interaction layers are derived from the current XIs and the tip’s ShapIQ budget. They are transparent what-if attributions, not a second full retrain per click.

Lineups & Best XI

Before tipping a matchday we resolve an XI per side. Prefer API-Football confirmed / last XIs when a key is set; otherwise published 2025–27 squad lists (footballsquads / Wikipedia) and an estimated eleven. Those player ratings go into the prediction features, then into the pitch lab.

Best XI rebuilds the side from the strongest available players (best GK + top outfield). The tip can move when the XI strength gap changes. If the board is already optimal you will see “already strongest by rating”.

Squad-list strengths are estimates, not Transfermarkt. Confirmed matchday lineups win when API-Football is configured.

Limits (be honest)

  • Tips are probabilities, not certainties — football is noisy.
  • Training uses historical results + engineered features; injuries, tactics, and referees are only partly captured.
  • Squad-estimate XIs can miss who actually starts on Saturday.
  • Lab swaps use a local sensitivity / ShapIQ approx — not a full model retrain on every click.
